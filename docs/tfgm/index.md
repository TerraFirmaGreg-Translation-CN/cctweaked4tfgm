# TerraFirmaGreg

<VersionPill show-core />

TerraFirmaGreg Modern does not fork CC: Tweaked. It takes the upstream JAR as a normal dependency and rewrites everything around it: recipes, progression gates, the creative tab, the quest chapter, and the item-name overrides. This guide separates the two layers so you can tell "this is CC:T" from "this is TFGM".

- [What TFGM changes](/tfgm/differences) — the full delta, item by item.
- [Recipes](/tfgm/recipe-design) — the GregTech rebuild of every CC:T recipe.
- [Turtle upgrades](/tfgm/turtle-upgrades) — 73 GT tool upgrades.
- [Progression](/tfgm/progression) — when computers become available, and what they cost.
- [Language & text](/tfgm/language) — UTF-8 support and the locale overrides.
- [Advanced Peripherals](/tfgm/advanced-peripherals) — the CC addon TFGM keeps, and the parts it removes.
- [Upstream & versioning](/tfgm/upstream) — CC:T release cadence and where TFGM sits.
- [Data pipeline](/tfgm/data-pipeline) — how this site is generated.
- [Integrations](/tfgm/integrations) — driving GregTech machines, Create blocks and Advanced Peripherals from Lua.
